# One-page operating and activation guide — DRAFT

## Use the pack with the existing workflow

1. Identify a real role or business with a specific reason to pursue it. In Contract Radar, track the sourced role or manually create a prospect. Multiple offer labels or undecided are appropriate until the need is understood.
2. Pick a relevant introduction. Verify the person, evidence, claims, contact source and timing; personalize a private copy. Exporting/editing does not create a record or send anything.
3. Send only after human review. Then manually record the actual contact/application date, current stage, and a real next action/date. Do not invent skipped events.
4. Use discovery notes to separate confirmed facts from unknowns. Replace the next action as the conversation develops; completing an action does not automatically advance stage.
5. Adapt one proposal/SOW to an actual scope. Resolve fees, parties, acceptance, dependencies and appropriate legal terms. Mark a proposal as delivered only after actually sending it.
6. Mark won only after acceptance. Enter confirmed active hours and dates explicitly; expected hours, a win, or a proposed fee do not reserve capacity. Billing and collected revenue remain separate records.
7. Review Follow-ups daily for overdue, due and unscheduled actions. Close or reopen intentionally while preserving history. Use actual counts and denominators when comparing offers.

## Human activation checklist

- Review service scope, proof attribution and availability copy. No unsupported client claims, exact private capacity or assumed market rates go public.
- Confirm the business mailbox {{email}} receives a human test; a mailto link does not prove delivery.
- Review and authorize the portfolio publication separately. Before release, confirm the Netlify project’s connected repository, production branch and configured canonical site origin; do not deploy as a discovery method.
- If approved later, configure the owned vanity domain and chosen www variant to redirect with valid TLS to the actual canonical consulting URL. Verify redirect behavior and avoid duplicate indexed pages. Do not change DNS from this pack.
- Once the page is live, update LinkedIn headline/About/Featured links and other profiles using reviewed copy. Review resume changes separately; preserve the approved PDF until then.
- Choose a small evidence-backed set of actual prospects across the three offers, personalize messages, send manually, and record real outcomes. A company listing a job is not proof of consulting budget.
- Agree client-specific commercial/legal terms before signing; no template is an executed agreement.

## Review after actual use

Compare real replies, qualified conversations, proposals and paid starts across offers. Keep denominators and dates. Use that evidence to revisit pricing hypotheses or workflow friction. Consider more automation only when repeated manual work has a measurable cost. No automatic sending, extraction, billing, signatures or revenue reporting is enabled by these files.
